# Play Store listing copy

Paste these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

Play's description fields are plain text: line breaks survive, HTML and Markdown
do not. The copy below is written that way — no asterisks, no tags — so it can
be pasted as-is.

Everything here describes features the app actually ships today. Premium is
deliberately described as per person, and only for what it does (no ads, no
daily coach limit). Nothing claims ratings, awards or "best", which Play's
metadata policy prohibits.

## Full description (4,000 characters max) — 2,376

```
Me&u is a shared space for two people. One hub, two phones, and the small habits that keep you close.

CHECK IN, EVERY DAY
Log your mood and social battery in a couple of taps. Your partner sees how your day is actually going, and your streak only grows when you both show up. When you want to reach out but don't know what to say, the nudge sheet opens the app you already use and suggests the line.

SETTLE THE SMALL STUFF
Who picks dinner? Who's being the bigger person? Flip a coin, spin a wheel you both fill in, or let the date setter choose when neither of you can. Swipe through films until you match on one. Write your own "how well do you know me?" questions and find out who was really listening.

NEVER FORGET THE DETAILS
Ring size, coffee order, allergies, the dream trip. You fill in theirs, they fill in yours, and your home screen shows how much you know about each other.

PLAN TOGETHER
A shared calendar with countdowns and reminders. A bucket list for everything you keep saying you should do someday. A gallery only the two of you can see.

A COACH THAT'S ONLY YOURS
Not sure how to start a difficult conversation? Ask an AI coach, privately. Your partner can never see it, and that's enforced on our servers rather than hidden in the app. Friendly guidance, not therapy, and not a crisis service.

BUILT FOR EXACTLY TWO
No feeds, no followers, no leaderboards. One six-letter invite code links your two phones. Check-ins, game results and nudges reach the other phone in seconds, which makes it work as well across time zones as it does across the sofa.

WHAT STAYS PRIVATE
Your to-dos and your coach conversations are yours alone. Check-ins, the calendar, the bucket list, the gallery, the details you record about each other and your game results are shared with your partner, and nobody else. We never sell what you write. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from the app or from our website.

FREE, WITH ONE SMALL OPTION
Everything above is free, with a few clearly labelled ads and three coach questions a day. Premium removes the ads and the daily limit for $1 a month. It is per person, so your partner doesn't have to subscribe for you to have it.

Me&u is for adults aged 18 and over.

Privacy policy: https://www.meandyou.tech/privacy/
Terms: https://www.meandyou.tech/terms/
Support: https://www.meandyou.tech/support/
```

## Notes

- **Premium** is described as $1 a month per person. Play shows local prices, so
  don't repeat the figure anywhere the currency could mislead. If billing isn't
  in the first release, cut the last paragraph's second and third sentences.
- **The AI coach** is called out as "not therapy, not a crisis service" in the
  listing as well as in-app, which is the claim Play and Apple both look for
  from anything offering relationship guidance.
- **Ads** are mentioned in the description and must also be declared in
  Play Console → App content → Ads.
- **18+** here has to match the target audience you select in App content.
